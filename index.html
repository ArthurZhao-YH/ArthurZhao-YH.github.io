About Me

renderer, and technical artist.

I am an MSc Computer Animation and Visual Effects student at Bournemouth University with interests in computer graphics, non-photorealistic rendering, real-time shaders, game development, animation, and visual effects.

My current work is shaped by both technical implementation and visual problem solving: building tools, studying rendering techniques, prototyping interactive scenes, and turning graphics ideas into usable experiences.

I enjoy projects where code needs to support artists and designers, especially workflows that combine engines, shaders, simulation, and production-friendly interfaces.
